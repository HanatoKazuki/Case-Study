Short Descriptions About Coffee

Latte

A latte blends bold espresso with rich steamed milk, topped with a delicate layer of silky microfoam.

Americano

An Americano combines bold espresso with hot water, creating a smooth, rich coffee similar in strength to drip brew.

Capuccino

A cappuccino features equal parts bold espresso, warm steamed milk, and a thick, airy layer of foam.

Short Descriptions About Tea

Oolong Tea

Oolong tea is a partially oxidized tea, balancing green tea's fresh notes with the rich depth of black tea.

Black Tea

Black tea is a fully oxidized tea, robust flavor with dark reddish-amber hues and high caffeine.

Green Tea

Green tea is an antioxidant-rich beverage made from unfermented Camellia sinensis leaves.

Why do people like Coffee or Tea?

Coffee Latte

Why Coffee?

People drink coffee for its boosting effects—caffeine enhances focus, energy, and alertness. Beyond the chemical lift, many enjoy its rich, complex flavors and the comforting daily ritual, whether as a morning wake-up or a social link.

Green Tea

Why Tea?

People drink tea for a gentler, sustained energy boost from caffeine combined with L-theanine, which promotes calm focus. It is also favored for its vast flavor profiles, health benefits like antioxidants, and relaxing, mindful brewing rituals.
